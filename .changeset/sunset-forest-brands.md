Add two more built-in brands: **Sunset** (orange) and **Forest** (emerald). Each is a
semantic-only overlay like Ocean — vibrant accent with a dark `text.on-accent` — and both
pass the WCAG AA contrast gate in light and dark. Switch with `data-brand="sunset"` /
`data-brand="forest"`; both are selectable from the Storybook Brand toolbar.
